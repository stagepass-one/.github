# Support

Stagepass is open source and maintained by a small team. There is no paid support or SLA, but we read everything.

## Where to get help

- **Docs:** [stagepass.one/docs](https://stagepass.one/docs/) covers the CLI, loader, modules, flag states, security and CORS.
- **Troubleshooting:** [stagepass.one/docs/troubleshooting](https://stagepass.one/docs/troubleshooting/) lists common setup problems and fixes.
- **Guides:** [stagepass.one/guides](https://stagepass.one/guides/) has practical articles on Webflow custom code and local development.
- **Bugs and feature requests:** [open an issue in stagepass-one/os](https://github.com/stagepass-one/os/issues/new/choose).
- **Security issues:** never in public. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Everything else:** [hello@stagepass.one](mailto:hello@stagepass.one).

## Helpful details

When you ask for help, include the versions of `@stagepass/cli`, `@stagepass/loader` or `@stagepass/modules`, your macOS and browser version, and the steps that lead to the problem. Leave out secrets, tokens and private URLs.
